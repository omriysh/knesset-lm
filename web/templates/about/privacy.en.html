Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Meorav Yerushalmi (meorav.com) is a personal, non-profit and non-commercial project operated by Omri Yeshaya (omri@meorav.com). This policy explains what data is collected when you use the website, the API or the MCP server, why, who receives it and how long it is kept. There are no sign-ups, user accounts, ads or tracking tools. Omri Yeshaya is the data controller, and the legal basis for processing is a legitimate interest in running and securing the service.

Use through MCP (ChatGPT, Claude and others)

  • When your chat app calls a Meorav Yerushalmi tool, the server receives only the tool name, the arguments the chat app chose to send (for example search words, a Knesset member's name or a meeting number), and technical request details.
  • The server does not receive your conversation, your name, your email address or your account details with the AI service. Requests usually come from the AI service's servers, so the IP address logged is theirs.
  • No authentication: the MCP server neither asks for nor receives passwords, tokens or permissions.
  • The conversation itself is governed by the privacy policy of the AI service you use.
  • Tool arguments are logged like any other request (below) and kept for 14 days.

What is collected

  • Technical details of each request: IP address, country (from Cloudflare), Cloudflare request id, time, page path and query parameters, response status, response time and size, browser user-agent, and error details if any.
  • Query content: questions asked in the in-site chat, arguments of tools called through MCP or the API, and search words on the Knesset protocols page.
  • In-site chat sessions: the question, plan, tool results and answer are kept on the server to show you the session, and are deleted shortly after.
  • The "היכרות" game: your choices stay in your browser. They are sent to the server only to compute the results, and are neither stored nor logged.
  • Gemini API key: kept only in your browser and sent to the server only to serve the request. The server does not store it; the log records only whether a key was sent and whether it worked.
  • In your browser (localStorage): your settings, and your Gemini key if you entered one. You can delete them in the settings or in your browser. The site itself sets no cookies; Cloudflare may set technical security cookies.
  • Emails: whatever you choose to write.

Please avoid personal details in questions and searches, since they are logged.

Purposes

  • Running the service and returning answers.
  • Security and abuse prevention, including rate limiting.
  • Finding and fixing bugs.
  • Improving search, tools and the instructions given to chat apps.
  • Aggregate usage statistics.

Recipients

  • Only I access the logs. They are not published, sold or passed to others, except as listed here: the infrastructure providers process them technically, and I may pass excerpts to AI services for analysis.
  • Bluehost: server hosting, where the logs are stored.
  • Cloudflare: the network layer all site traffic passes through. Cloudflare may store and analyze traffic data under its own policies, which I do not control.
  • Google (Gemini API): in-site chat only. Your question and the data gathered for it are sent to Gemini with your own key, under Google's terms for your account.
  • AI services (for example Anthropic's Claude): I may pass log excerpts to them to analyze for debugging and improving the service. These services may store this data and use it to train their models, under their terms.
  • The Knesset: some tools send search words and other arguments to the Knesset's public API. No identifying data about you is sent.
  • The public: I may publish aggregate usage statistics (for example totals of requests or questions), but never a single request or details that identify who sent it. Until after the elections I will not publish aggregates about parties or candidates, including "היכרות" game results.
  • When required by law.
  • Some of these providers process data outside Israel, including in the US.

Retention

  • My server logs: 14 days, then deleted automatically. Cloudflare and Bluehost keep their own logs under their own policies.
  • In-site chat sessions: up to 2 hours.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle the request.

Your controls

  • You can use the site without asking the in-site chat anything and without entering a key.
  • You can delete your Gemini key and settings from the settings dialog.
  • You can ask to access or delete log data about you, under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, at omri@meorav.com. Include an approximate time and details that help me find your requests.

Minors

There is no minimum age, but the site is not directed at children under 13. The site does not ask for personal details, and I recommend not entering any in questions.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and only I access the server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with its date.
