Who am I?

My name is Omri Yeshaya, I'm 27, originally from a moshav in the south of Israel, and today I live and work in Tel Aviv.

This site has been my personal project for the past few months. I built it in my free time, with no funding from anyone and no ties to any political or non-political body.

Ahead of the elections, I wanted to dig in and read what our Knesset members actually work on today, and to decide who to vote for based on what is said in the Knesset rather than on social media or in the news. A lot of information is public, but not in a convenient form, and no platform gave me what I wanted to read. That's why I built this site. I hope it helps you as much as it helped me!

I'd love to hear your feedback and thoughts, or just tell you more about the project and talk politics and technology. Get in touch.

Reporting a content error

The summaries, topics and opinions on the site were written by a language model and can be wrong. If you find an opinion attributed to the wrong person, a quote that does not support its opinion, or a misidentified Knesset member, send me the link to the row (every result carries a link to its protocol) and what you believe is wrong.

What to include

  • The link to the page or result.
  • What you expected and what happened.
  • The browser or chat app you used (ChatGPT, Claude, the in-site chat).
